# Hands-on test: a publisher and a UX designer (2026-09-26)

Two Opus agents used Nassakh through Chrome like real users while the owner slept: a **publisher** (take books
all the way to Word / PDF / EPUB) and a **UX designer** (navigation, consistency, feedback, tools, accessibility).
Books: four scanned Arabic books picked at random from `~/Documents/Books`, cut short so processing stays quick.

| Book | Id | Pages | Tester | How far |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| المنهل العذب في تاريخ طرابلس الغرب (ص 67–74) | 23 | 8 | publisher | reviewed, book, edited, 4 exports |
| سيرة الأستاذ جوذر (ص 34–41), a 96 dpi scan | 24 | 8 | publisher | processed, book built from unreviewed pages (to test) |
| الحوليات الليبية (whole 555-page PDF, skip 186 / 362) | 25 | 7 | publisher | reviewed, book, 4 exports |
| أيسر الشروح على متن الآجرومية (ص 87–94) | 26 | 8 | UX | reviewed, book, edited, Word + print PDF |

Timings: first page ready 40 s – 2 min after «بدء المعالجة»; a whole 8-page book in 4–6 min (three books at
once); relayout after a trim or font change 2–4 s; exports 0.5–4 s each.

## What works well (both testers)
- Processing is fast, and review can start while the rest of the book is still being read.
- **Review is the strongest screen**: the word popover with numbered readings, Tab to the next uncertain word,
  the scan highlighting the word, undo toasts, the «?» shortcuts sheet.
- **The book page**: live pages close to print, trim and fonts in a few clicks, editing on the page with
  autosave, the style picker, real footnotes (⌘⇧F), find & replace with Arabic options, «الأصل» with the scan.
- **The export page**: clear, every option explained, fast; the files are good (Word styles, real footnotes,
  contents field, Amiri embedded; PDF bleed and crop marks correct; EPUB right to left).
- A calm, consistent look; no console errors in the whole session.

## Blockers
1. **A review fix after the book exists wipes the book-page work.** «إعادة تجميع الفصل من المراجعة» (book page
   «⋯») replaces the whole chapter at once, with no confirmation: headings, footnotes and word fixes made on the
   book page are lost (UX: 8 → 6 pages). The drift banner appears only after a reload. The manuscript's own
   regroup warns properly («استبدال النص المحرَّر»); the book page's does not. (Both confirmed.)
2. **Restore and undo undid themselves — fixed the same morning.** The undo toast's button was shown with
   `x-show="…bookToast.action"`; Alpine calls a function an expression evaluates to, so showing the toast ran
   the undo: every snapshot restore, digit conversion, replace-all and separator delete was reverted at once.
   Now the button is bound to a boolean (`hasAction`), with a Node test (`core/test_layout_ui.py`).
3. **Review shortcuts fail with the Arabic keyboard layout** (A / E / N / ? and 1–3): proofreaders type in
   Arabic, and with a word selected an Arabic letter even starts a correction. The book page already matches
   the physical key (ث, س, ل work there). (Both.)
4. **OCR trust.** A whole printed line was dropped without any flag (book 23 p. 1); pages with «0» uncertain
   words had a dozen real errors, while about 70 % of book 25's flags were correct punctuation or digits;
   dates came out wrong and unflagged. "0 uncertain" is read as "proofread". (Publisher, UX saw the same.)

## Major
**Navigation between stages**
- No stage bar. Review links only to «لوحة الكتاب»; after the last page nothing says what comes next; from the
  dashboard, export is only in «⋯»; a review opened from the book page returns to the dashboard, not the book.
- Names: «تحويل إلى كتاب» builds the manuscript; «الكتاب» means both the project and the typeset book;
  «يُخرَج الكتاب…» (relayout) collides with «الإخراج» (export).
- Grid thumbnails on the dashboard open the technical page detail, not review.
- `?tab=` deep links open the right tab but are then removed from the address (a copied link loses the tab).

**Defaults and checks**
- «تضمين الصفحات غير المُراجَعة» is checked by default (dashboard and manuscript popovers): one click builds a
  book from raw OCR (book 24).
- «قبل الإخراج» does not list unreviewed pages or stray «(1) …» footnote paragraphs, and says «جاهز».
- The upload form never shows which pages the skip values leave (555 pages, 186 / 362 → 7); a typo would queue
  hundreds of pages. Processing needs a second click («بدء المعالجة»).
- Progress shows two measures in one place («0 من 8 صفحة» and a 67 % bar) and no time estimate.

**Structure**
- Footnote detection is weak (book 25: 1 of 6 footnotes found; one merged with a body line); there is no
  footnote role in review or in the manuscript's paragraph menu.
- Verse: hemistichs merged in pairs into the wrong paragraphs; «شعر» only centres the line.

**Tools and screens**
- The page detail is an engineer's screen (crop box in pixels, Sauvola settings, engine runs) shown to everyone;
  re-run actions sit in the same «⋯» as navigation, without confirmation.
- The book page's seven side tabs are icons only; snapshots, digit conversion and the preview PDF are in «⋯».
- The screen PDF's text layer is garbled with Traditional Arabic (copied text reads «اإلسالمي»): search and copy
  break for that face (Amiri is fine).
- Narrow widths: review at 800 px leaves a 200 px text pane; at 420 px its header overflows and «اعتماد الصفحة»
  is cut off; the book page's toolbar overlaps at 420 px.
- Contrast: the muted grey (#8A8A93, 3.4:1) is used for 12 px hints, below 4.5:1; over 100 rules use 10–12.5 px.

## Minor (selection)
English file picker («Choose File»); «8 صفحة» in a toast; the grid/list choice is shared by all books; the
«تحتاج انتباهًا» list never clears after approval and does not say which line; after «A» moves to the next page,
«N» skips one; the same key means different things on different screens (1/2/3, 0, E, S); Latin digits in
Times look large next to 13 pt Traditional Arabic; «استثناء الصفحة» has no undo; the UX tester saw «Øµ» for «ص»
in page markers once (not reproduced: Chrome decodes the stylesheet correctly).

## Top five, merged
1. **A safe round trip**: the book page's regroup gets the manuscript's warning and a "only the changed pages"
   choice; the drift banner shows live. (Restore and undo: fixed.)
2. **Arabic keyboard**: physical-key shortcuts in review and on the dashboard, ١–٩ accepted, one meaning per key.
3. **A stage bar** on every book screen (المراجعة · المخطوطة · الكتاب · الإخراج, with done / current / out of
   date), an end-of-review next step, and review returning to where it was opened from.
4. **Trust and readiness**: detect dropped lines, stop flagging plain punctuation, a book-wide "fix this
   misreading everywhere", readiness listing unreviewed pages and stray footnotes, the unreviewed-pages box off
   by default.
5. **Structure**: a footnote role and better detection, verse with صدر / عجز.
Then the polish: names, the upload range preview and auto-start, labelled tabs, contrast, narrow widths, the
screen PDF text layer with Traditional Arabic.
